Foundations is a minimalist survival overhaul built to bring deliberate friction back to your playthroughs.

Instead of cluttering the game, it surgically reshapes core mechanics to restore weight, danger, and consequence to the world.
  • Light is no longer free.
  • Progression demands descent.
  • Time hardens the world.
Inspired by the Pre-Beta 1.8 era, Foundations applies the Pareto Principle: minimal changes that radically transform how the game is learned, explored, and survived.

⚠️ Not for casual builders. Foundations is a slower survival-first experience.
